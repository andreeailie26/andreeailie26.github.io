    My Experience

    At the Conservatorium van Amsterdam, I worked as a university assistant in the theory department, supporting students in understanding complex musical concepts. What I enjoyed most wasn’t just the content itself, but the process of teaching and explaining — finding the right way to make something “click” for each person.

    Most of my work focused on harmony and analysis of classical music, from Baroque to Modernism in the interbelic period. When needed, I also brought in elements of music history and general theory to give more context, especially when it helped connect abstract ideas to something more tangible.

    I really liked working one-on-one with students or in small groups, guiding them through difficult material, and seeing their confidence grow over time. That experience made me realize how much I enjoy coaching and helping people develop, especially when it comes to breaking down complex ideas into something clear and manageable.
