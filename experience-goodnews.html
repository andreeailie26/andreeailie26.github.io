    My Experience

    I started at GoodNews as a barista and quickly grew into a management role, where I got to work on both the people and the operations side of the store.

    What I enjoyed most was building a team, training new staff, and finding ways to improve how things worked day to day. From handling busy shifts to solving small (and sometimes unexpected) problems, it taught me how much impact good structure and clear communication can have.

    I also took part in interviewing new team members and thinking carefully about who would fit not just the role, but the team itself. Over time, I became more focused on creating an environment where people actually wanted to come to work — where expectations were clear, communication was open, and people felt supported. This had a noticeable impact on attendance and overall team energy, and showed me how much the right environment can influence performance.
